# Information Security Policy Documented and Communicated (OIS-01) -- consultant-furnished context

## Verdict (consultant-asserted)

outcome: PARTIAL
severity: high
rationale: >-
  An information security policy document exists and has been approved by
  the CISO. However, the policy has not been formally reviewed since its
  initial publication 18 months ago, and there is no recorded acknowledgement
  from all staff confirming they have read it. Distribution was via email
  broadcast only, with no tracking of receipt. The policy partially satisfies
  OIS-01; the review cycle and documented communication trail requirements
  are not yet in place.

## Policy register

| Requirement | Current state | Gap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Written information security policy exists | Present (v1.2, published 2025-01) | No gap |
| Management approval documented | CISO signed off | No gap |
| Communicated to all internal users | Email only, no tracking | Yes |
| Communicated to relevant external users | Not addressed | Yes |
| Review cycle defined and executed | No review since publication | Yes |
| Policy covers objectives, scope, and governance framework | Present | No gap |

## Counter-hypothesis considered

Considered whether the email broadcast and intranet publication constitute
sufficient communication for OIS-01. Rejected: the criterion requires
evidenced communication with confirmation, not broadcast only. The absence
of any external-user communication and the expired review cycle create
two distinct gaps.
