# Incident Response Plan and Team Defined (INM-01) -- consultant-furnished context

## Verdict (consultant-asserted)

outcome: PARTIAL
severity: high
rationale: >-
  An incident response plan document exists and names an incident response
  team (IRT) with four named members from Security Operations, Legal, and
  IT Operations. The plan covers severity classification, escalation paths,
  and initial containment steps. However, the plan has never been formally
  tested through a tabletop or simulation exercise. Communication procedures
  for customer notification are absent. The plan was last reviewed 20 months
  ago. The control partially satisfies INM-01; the testing requirement and
  customer notification procedures are outstanding.

## Counter-hypothesis considered

Considered whether the existence of a documented plan without testing
satisfies INM-01. Rejected: BSI C5:2020 requires that the plan be tested
at regular intervals. An untested plan cannot provide reasonable confidence
that the defined procedures will function under actual incident conditions.
