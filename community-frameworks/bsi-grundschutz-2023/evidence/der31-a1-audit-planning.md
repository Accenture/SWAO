# Internal Audit Planning (DER.3.1.A1) -- evidence template

## Purpose

Information security audits must be planned and conducted at defined intervals, covering all components in the IT-Grundschutz modelling scope; findings must be tracked with assigned owners and remediated within defined timeframes.

## Evidence required

- Annual audit schedule covering all in-scope components, showing planned audit dates and responsible parties
- Qualifications and independence of assigned auditors, confirmed through CVs, certifications, or an independence declaration
- Most recent audit report, demonstrating that the scope and objectives defined in the schedule were met
- Finding register with assigned owners and target remediation dates, showing the current status of each open finding
- Evidence that findings from the previous audit cycle were tracked to closure, such as a comparison of the prior finding register against closure records
