# Compliance Monitoring (ORP.5.A2) -- evidence template

## Purpose

Regular checks must be performed to confirm that legal, regulatory, and policy requirements are being met; non-compliance findings must be tracked with defined remediation timescales and results reported to management.

## Evidence required

- Compliance calendar listing all scheduled check activities with assigned owners and the frequency of each check
- Results or report from the most recent compliance check cycle, covering the areas and requirements in scope
- Management report presenting the current compliance posture, dated within the most recent reporting period
- Gap or finding register listing all open non-compliance findings with assigned owners and defined remediation timescales
- Evidence that findings are tracked to closure, such as a closed-finding log or status history showing resolution dates for previously open items
