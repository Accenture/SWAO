# IT Forensics Capability for Incident Investigation (DER.2.1.A4) -- evidence template

## Purpose

The institution must maintain or have contractual access to IT forensics capability; a forensic evidence preservation procedure must exist and first-responder staff must be trained in evidence handling.

## Evidence required

- Description or contract for IT forensics capability, identifying the internal team or external provider and their scope of service
- Documented forensic evidence preservation procedure, covering chain of custody, approved tools, and steps to avoid evidence contamination
- Training records for first-responder staff covering evidence preservation, with training date and content scope documented
- Evidence of at least one dry-run exercise to test forensic capability, with exercise date, scenario, and outcome on file
- Contact list for invoking forensics capability in an incident, confirmed as current and accessible to incident response staff
