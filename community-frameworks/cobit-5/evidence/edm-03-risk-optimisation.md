# EDM03: Ensure Risk Optimisation

Sample evidence for illustration; replace it with the client's own findings.

## Verdict (consultant-asserted)

outcome: PARTIAL
severity: high
rationale: >-
  The client's risk appetite statement, set in 2025-Q4, includes IT limits
  for confidentiality, availability and dependence on single suppliers. IT
  risks sit in the enterprise risk register and are reviewed every quarter.
  The gap: the statement says nothing about AI or model risk, and the AI
  workloads planned for 2026-Q3 would exceed what the board has implicitly
  accepted. Action INC-RISK-0142 is open to close this. Capability is rated
  Level 2 (managed); the AI gap prevents Level 3.

## Evidence references

- ADR-RISK-APP-2025: enterprise risk appetite statement
- Enterprise risk register, 2026-Q1 export
- INC-RISK-0142: open action to extend the appetite to AI and model risk
- Board minutes 2025-Q4: risk appetite endorsement, item 6.1

## Counter-hypothesis considered

Is the gap only informational, because the board was briefed and accepted
the remaining risk for the 2026-Q3 launch? Rejected: the board made its
acceptance conditional on the appetite being extended before launch.
