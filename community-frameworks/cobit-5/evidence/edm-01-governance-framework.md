# EDM01: Ensure Governance Framework Setting and Maintenance

Sample evidence for illustration; replace it with the client's own findings.

## Verdict (consultant-asserted)

outcome: SATISFIED
severity: high
rationale: >-
  The board approved an IT governance charter on 2025-09-12 and reviewed it
  again at its 2026-Q1 meeting. The charter names three decision bodies:
  the IT Steering Committee, the CIO and the Enterprise Risk Committee. A
  decision-rights matrix assigns strategy, portfolio, security and
  operations decisions to them. The policy register sets out a four-level
  hierarchy (policy, standard, procedure, work instruction). Capability is
  rated Level 3 (established).

## Evidence references

- ADR-IT-GOV-001: IT governance charter (board-approved, 2025-09-12)
- Policy register PR-2026-Q1: 47 policies with the hierarchy recorded
- Board minutes 2026-Q1: governance review, item 4.2
- RACI-IT-DECISION-RIGHTS v3.1

## Counter-hypothesis considered

Does the steering committee only discuss operational matters, which would
weaken the governance claim? Rejected: the 2026-Q1 agenda covered the
strategy refresh, confirmation of risk appetite and a review of realised
benefits, all of which are governance-level topics.
