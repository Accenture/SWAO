# Roles and Responsibilities for Information Security (ORP.1.A1) -- consultant-furnished context

## Verdict (consultant-asserted)

outcome: PARTIAL
severity: high
rationale: >-
  The platform team has a shared understanding of who is responsible for
  operational security tasks, but this has not been formalised in a documented
  RACI or equivalent role definition. The information security officer role
  exists but covers the whole organisation; no app-level information security
  responsibilities are assigned in writing. Incident and continuity roles are
  particularly underspecified: team members acknowledged in workshop that they
  would not know who to escalate to in a major security incident outside
  business hours. The gap is the absence of documented, communicated role
  definitions -- not the absence of capable staff.

## Role coverage register

| Security responsibility | Owner identified | Documented | Communicated formally |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Information security officer (organisation) | Yes | Informal | No |
| App-level security champion | Not assigned | No | No |
| Incident response lead | Not assigned | No | No |
| Data protection contact | Shared with legal team | Informal | No |
| Change authority (security-impacting changes) | CTO ad hoc | No | No |
| Business continuity lead | Not identified | No | No |

## Counter-hypothesis considered

Considered whether the team's operational familiarity with security responsibilities
satisfies the ORP.1.A1 Basis requirement. Rejected: BSI requires roles to be
documented and communicated to all relevant staff. Implicit knowledge held by
tenured team members does not satisfy the requirement, particularly for
continuity coverage during staff turnover.
