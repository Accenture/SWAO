# Post-Change Evaluation (OPS.1.1.2.A4) -- evidence template

## Purpose

After completing a change, a formal evaluation must confirm objectives were met and no unintended side-effects occurred; security-relevant changes must include a specific security verification step.

## Evidence required

- Post-change evaluation records for a sample of recent changes, showing objectives, outcomes, and any identified side-effects
- Evidence that the security verification step was completed for security-relevant changes, with the verifier named separately from the implementer
- Independent reviewer sign-off on post-change evaluation, confirming the reviewer is separate from the change implementer
- Change records showing the evaluation outcome, including confirmation of closure or listing of outstanding actions
- Evidence of at least one post-change action taken to address an identified side-effect, showing the issue was tracked to resolution
