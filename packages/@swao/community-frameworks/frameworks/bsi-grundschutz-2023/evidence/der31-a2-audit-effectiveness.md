# Auditing Information Security Measures (DER.3.1.A2) -- evidence template

## Purpose

Implemented security measures must be regularly audited for effectiveness through both documentary review and technical testing; results must be reported to management and fed back into the security programme.

## Evidence required

- Most recent audit report covering both documentary review and technical testing elements, confirming that both methods were applied
- Evidence of penetration test or configuration review conducted in the current audit cycle, including the test scope and date
- Management report presenting audit results at an appropriate level of detail, with a record of the meeting or distribution at which it was presented
- Management sign-off on the remediation plan arising from the audit, showing acceptance of the plan and the named accountable owner
- Confirmation that technical test findings are tracked in the remediation workflow, with links between test findings and remediation register entries
