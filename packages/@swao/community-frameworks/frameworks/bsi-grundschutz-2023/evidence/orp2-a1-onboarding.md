# Regulated Procedures for Onboarding New Staff (ORP.2.A1) -- evidence template

## Purpose

A defined onboarding process must ensure that new staff, contractors, and temporary staff receive access rights, equipment, and security briefings only after identity verification and role assignment are complete.

## Evidence required

- Joiner checklist showing an explicit gate that requires role approval and identity verification before access provisioning is initiated
- Identity verification records for a sample of recent joiners confirming that the verification step was completed and documented before access was granted
- Evidence that contractors and temporary staff follow the same onboarding process as permanent employees, or an equivalent process with the same gating requirements
- Signed security acknowledgement or acceptable-use agreement records for recent joiners showing that the briefing was completed
- Confirmation from provisioning logs or the joiner checklist that access is not provisioned before the identity verification step completes
