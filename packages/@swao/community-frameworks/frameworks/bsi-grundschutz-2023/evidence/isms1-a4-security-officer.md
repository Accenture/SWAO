# Appointment of an Information Security Officer (ISMS.1.A4) -- evidence template

## Purpose

An information security officer (ISO) must be formally appointed with sufficient authority, time, and access to ensure the information security process is established and maintained across the institution.

## Evidence required

- Written appointment document naming the ISO, signed by senior management, with an effective date
- Documented scope of authority confirming the ISO has the right to issue binding security requirements across all organisational units
- Organisational chart or reporting-line document showing the ISO's reporting relationship to management and independence from operational IT management
- Evidence of adequate time allocation for ISO duties, such as a role description specifying the percentage of working time dedicated to the function
- Record of the most recent management review or briefing at which the ISO reported on the information security posture
