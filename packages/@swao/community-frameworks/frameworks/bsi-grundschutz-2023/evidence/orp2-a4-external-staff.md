# Regulated Use of External Staff and Contractors (ORP.2.A4) -- evidence template

## Purpose

External personnel must be subject to information security requirements equivalent to internal staff, must sign non-disclosure agreements before access is granted, and must have access limited to the minimum necessary for their contracted task.

## Evidence required

- Non-disclosure agreement register with signed NDA records on file for all currently active contractors and external personnel
- Register of active contractors listing each individual's access scope and confirming that scope is limited to their contracted task
- Evidence that access provisioned for contractors does not exceed the minimum necessary, such as access request records showing the scope justification
- Records of access review conducted during long-running engagements confirming that access remains appropriate and scoped to current contractual need
- Evidence of access revocation on contract end for a sample of recently completed engagements, including revocation date relative to contract end date
