# Assignment of Tasks and Authorisations (ORP.1.A2) -- evidence template

## Purpose

Tasks and authorisations must be assigned consistently, documented, and follow least privilege. No individual should hold both operational and audit responsibilities for a critical process; access rights must be reviewed periodically.

## Evidence required

- Documented task and role assignment register mapping each role to its responsibilities and the authorisations granted
- Evidence of a periodic access rights review including the review date, the reviewer, and the scope of systems reviewed
- Privileged access provisioning log showing approvals and the justification recorded at the time of provisioning
- Segregation-of-duties matrix for critical processes confirming that no single individual holds conflicting operational and audit rights
- Records of access rights removed or adjusted at the most recent periodic review, including the reason for each change
