# Firewall Security Policy (NET.3.2.A1) -- evidence template

## Purpose

A documented firewall security policy must define the default-deny principle, with permitted traffic flows explicitly allowed on a need-to-communicate basis; the policy must be reviewed when requirements change and the ruleset audited annually.

## Evidence required

- Approved firewall security policy document establishing a default-deny baseline, with owner sign-off and version history
- Firewall ruleset mapped to the written policy, showing each permit rule is traceable to a documented business or technical requirement
- Most recent annual ruleset audit record, with findings listed and remediation actions tracked
- Evidence of rules removed or tightened following the last audit, such as a change record referencing the audit finding
- Process records showing policy review was triggered when application requirements changed, with before-and-after rule comparison
