# Formal Notification of Staff Obligations (ORP.1.A5) -- evidence template

## Purpose

All staff must be formally notified of information security obligations in writing and must acknowledge receipt and understanding; acknowledgements must be retained and refreshed at defined intervals.

## Evidence required

- Security obligations notification document issued to all staff, covering policies, acceptable use, and consequences of non-compliance
- Signed acknowledgement register covering all current employees and active contractors, with no outstanding gaps
- Evidence that acknowledgement is captured at onboarding as part of the joiner process, such as an HR workflow record
- Records of the most recent annual refresh of acknowledgements, showing the refresh date and completion rate
- Confirmation that the acknowledgement scope covers all required elements: policies, acceptable use, and consequences of non-compliance
