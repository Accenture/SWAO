# Regulated Procedures for Offboarding Departing Staff (ORP.2.A2) -- evidence template

## Purpose

A defined offboarding process must ensure that all access rights -- including credentials, tokens, certificates, and physical access -- are revoked promptly when staff leave or change role, applied equally to permanent and temporary staff.

## Evidence required

- Leaver checklist covering all access categories: logical system access, physical access, credentials, tokens, and certificates
- Revocation log with timestamps for a sample of recent leavers confirming that all access types were revoked and recording the date of revocation for each
- Evidence of automated or triggered deprovisioning initiated by the HR termination event, such as an identity platform workflow record or integration log
- Confirmation that revocation was completed within the defined SLA, measured against the employment end date or role-change date
- Evidence that temporary staff and contractors are subject to the same offboarding controls as permanent employees
