# Supervisory Responsibility for Information Security (ORP.1.A3) -- evidence template

## Purpose

Supervisors must ensure their staff fulfil security obligations, address non-compliance promptly, and participate themselves in security awareness activities.

## Evidence required

- Supervisor security responsibilities included in job descriptions or line manager agreements, confirming the obligation is formally assigned
- Records of supervisor participation in security awareness sessions within the past twelve months
- Documented example of a supervisor addressing staff security non-compliance, including the nature of the issue and outcome
- Supervisor confirmation that their team has completed mandatory security training, evidenced by a signed checklist or system record
- Evidence of a supervisory oversight process for security obligation fulfilment, such as a periodic check-in or reporting mechanism
