# Network Path Redundancy for Critical Infrastructure (NET.1.1.A4) -- evidence template

## Purpose

Critical network paths must be designed with redundancy; single points of failure must be documented; failover mechanisms must be tested at defined intervals.

## Evidence required

- Network topology diagram identifying all redundant paths and any remaining single points of failure
- Risk register entries for unresolved single points of failure, with a named owner and accepted or planned mitigation for each
- Most recent failover test results and test date, confirming all critical paths were included in the test scope
- Evidence that failover testing covered all critical paths without exception, such as a test scope checklist cross-referenced to the topology diagram
- Confirmation that the topology is reviewed after significant infrastructure changes, evidenced by a post-change review record or change management gate
