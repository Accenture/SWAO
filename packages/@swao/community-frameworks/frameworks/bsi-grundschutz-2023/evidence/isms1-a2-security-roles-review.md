# Review of Information Security Roles and Responsibilities (ISMS.1.A2) -- evidence template

## Purpose

Information security roles, responsibilities, and delegated authorities must be reviewed at least annually to verify they remain appropriate as the organisation evolves.

## Evidence required

- Annual roles review documentation with sign-off date confirming review was completed within the past twelve months
- RACI or responsibilities matrix version-controlled with change history showing current and prior versions
- Written re-acceptance of responsibilities by current role holders, dated within the review cycle
- Evidence of a review triggered by an organisational change, if one occurred in the past year
- ISO endorsement of the current roles assignment, documenting any adjustments made as a result of the review
