# Backup and Recovery Concept (OPS.1.1.5.A1) -- evidence template

## Purpose

A comprehensive backup and recovery concept must define scope, schedules, retention periods, media types, and recovery procedures for all in-scope systems, aligned with RPO and RTO requirements, and reviewed at defined intervals.

## Evidence required

- Approved backup and recovery concept document with version history and owner sign-off
- Confirmation that scope covers all in-scope systems, with a mapping to the asset inventory
- Evidence that RPO and RTO requirements from the backup requirements analysis are referenced and met by the defined schedules
- Operational backup configuration aligned with the concept, showing schedule and retention settings
- Most recent concept review date and reviewer identity, with any changes resulting from the review
