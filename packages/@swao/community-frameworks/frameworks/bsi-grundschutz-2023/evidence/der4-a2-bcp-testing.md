# Business Continuity Testing (DER.4.A2) -- evidence template

## Purpose

Business continuity and disaster recovery plans must be tested at defined intervals to verify recovery objectives can be met; results must be documented and used to improve the plan, and critical staff must be trained on their continuity roles.

## Evidence required

- Most recent BCP test record showing the date, scope, scenario used, and outcome against defined recovery objectives
- Documented gaps identified during the test and their remediation status, with assigned owners and target closure dates
- Evidence that critical recovery personnel have been briefed on their roles, such as training records or role acknowledgement forms
- Current contact directory for continuity events, including escalation paths and out-of-hours contact details, confirmed as reviewed within the last 12 months
- Confirmation that test findings fed back into an updated version of the BCP, with a version comparison showing the changes made
