# Information Security Policy (ISMS.1.A3) -- consultant-furnished context

## Verdict (consultant-asserted)

outcome: PARTIAL
severity: high
rationale: >-
  An information security policy document exists (`governance/security-policy.md`
  in the source repository) and was reviewed by the CTO. The document defines the
  scope and the general commitment to information security. However, it has not
  been reviewed since its initial publication (over 18 months ago), does not carry
  an explicit management sign-off date, and lacks a documented review cycle. The
  policy does not reference the applicability of BSI IT-Grundschutz as the
  governing methodology. Staff acknowledgement of the policy is informal and not
  recorded. The gap is documentation discipline rather than the absence of a
  security policy.
