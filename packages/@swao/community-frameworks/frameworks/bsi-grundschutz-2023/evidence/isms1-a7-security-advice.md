# Information Security Advice for Projects and Procurements (ISMS.1.A7) -- evidence template

## Purpose

The information security officer must be consulted on all projects and procurements with a material impact on information security, providing formal sign-off before key decisions are made.

## Evidence required

- ISO consultation process or gate checklist applied at project initiation, showing the trigger criteria for mandatory ISO involvement
- Sample ISO assessment or sign-off from at least one recent project or procurement, demonstrating the consultation in practice
- List of projects and procurements assessed by the ISO in the current year, with dates and outcomes
- Documented security requirements arising from at least one ISO review, showing the output of the consultation
- Confirmation that no project bypassed the ISO consultation requirement, evidenced by gate records or a signed attestation
