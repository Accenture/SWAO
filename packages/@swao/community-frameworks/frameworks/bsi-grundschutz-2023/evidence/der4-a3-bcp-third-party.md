# Business Continuity Testing with Third Parties (DER.4.A3) -- evidence template

## Purpose

Business continuity tests must include key third-party providers and supply chain partners; the joint test must verify their recovery capabilities and communication procedures.

## Evidence required

- List of critical third-party dependencies identified in the BCP, confirming they are in scope for continuity testing
- Evidence that at least one critical third party participated in the most recent annual continuity test, such as an attendance record or joint test report
- Joint test report documenting third-party participation, tested scenarios, and outcomes including any recovery time gaps identified
- Gaps identified from the joint exercise and their remediation status, with a named owner and target resolution date for each
- Contractual or agreement basis for third-party participation in continuity testing, confirming the obligation is formalised
