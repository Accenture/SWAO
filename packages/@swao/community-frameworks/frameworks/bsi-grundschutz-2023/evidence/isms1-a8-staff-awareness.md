# Integrating Staff into the Security Process (ISMS.1.A8) -- evidence template

## Purpose

All staff must receive mandatory security awareness training at onboarding and at regular intervals, and must know the escalation path for security incidents and their responsibilities during incident response.

## Evidence required

- Awareness training programme description including delivery schedule, topics covered, and the frequency of recurring sessions
- Completion records for onboarding security briefings showing that all new starters receive training before or immediately upon being granted access
- Annual refresher completion log with a list of staff covered and the date of completion for the most recent cycle
- Evidence that contractors and third-party personnel with access to systems or data are included in the awareness programme
- Documented escalation path for security incidents that has been communicated to all staff, with evidence of that communication such as an announcement record or intranet posting
