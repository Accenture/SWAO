# APO10: Manage Suppliers

Sample evidence for illustration; replace it with the client's own findings.

## Verdict (consultant-asserted)

outcome: PARTIAL
severity: high
rationale: >-
  The client keeps a register of 124 active suppliers, each with a
  criticality rating, contract dates and a named contact. All 29 suppliers
  rated critical or high have signed master agreements that include
  service levels, exit terms and a right to audit. Two gaps remain. First,
  17 medium-rated suppliers are working on expired contracts while renewals
  are negotiated. Second, dependence on a few large providers is reviewed
  once a year, whereas DORA Art. 28 expects it to be watched continuously.
  Both items sit in the PROG-TPRM-2026 programme. Capability is rated
  Level 2 (managed); Level 3 is expected once the third-party risk tooling
  is live.

## Evidence references

- Supplier register (SR-2026-Q1): 124 active suppliers
- Master agreements folder (legal): 29 of 29 critical suppliers signed
- PROG-TPRM-2026: third-party risk management programme (in progress)
- DORA Article 28 mapping note (compliance team)

## Counter-hypothesis considered

Could a yearly concentration review be enough because the supplier base
changes little? Rejected: a SaaS migration planned for 2026-Q2 will move a
large share of spend to two hyperscalers, and continuous monitoring would
have shown that shift sooner.
