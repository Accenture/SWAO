# APO10 -- Manage Suppliers

## Verdict (consultant-asserted)

outcome: PARTIAL
severity: high
rationale: >-
  A supplier register covers 124 active suppliers with criticality tier,
  contract dates and primary contact. Critical and high-criticality
  suppliers (29) have signed master agreements with the required SLAs,
  exit clauses and audit rights. Gaps: (i) 17 medium-criticality suppliers
  have expired contracts being renewed; (ii) concentration analysis is
  performed annually rather than continuously -- DORA Art. 28 expects
  ongoing monitoring of ICT third-party concentration. Treatment open
  under PROG-TPRM-2026. COBIT 5 process capability assessed at Level 2
  (Managed) with movement toward Level 3 once the TPRM tooling lands.

## Evidence references

- Supplier register (SR-2026-Q1) -- 124 active suppliers
- Master agreements folder (legal) -- 29/29 critical signed
- PROG-TPRM-2026 -- third-party risk management programme (in flight)
- DORA Article 28 mapping note (compliance team)

## Counter-hypothesis considered

Considered whether the annual concentration review is sufficient given
the supplier portfolio's stability; ruled out -- a new cloud SaaS migration
in 2026-Q2 will materially shift concentration toward two hyperscalers,
which a continuous monitor would have flagged earlier.
