# EDM03 -- Ensure Risk Optimisation

## Verdict (consultant-asserted)

outcome: PARTIAL
severity: high
rationale: >-
  An enterprise risk appetite statement exists and includes IT-specific
  tolerances for confidentiality, availability and supplier concentration
  (set in 2025-Q4). IT risks are recorded in the enterprise risk register
  and reviewed quarterly. Gap: the risk-appetite statement does not yet
  cover AI / model-risk dimensions, which the AI workloads landing in
  2026-Q3 would breach the implicit tolerance for; treatment is open under
  INC-RISK-0142. COBIT 5 process capability assessed at Level 2 (Managed
  process); Level 3 blocked by the AI-risk gap.

## Evidence references

- ADR-RISK-APP-2025 -- Enterprise risk appetite statement
- Enterprise risk register 2026-Q1 export
- INC-RISK-0142 -- open action to extend appetite to AI / model risk
- Board minutes 2025-Q4 -- risk appetite endorsement (item 6.1)

## Counter-hypothesis considered

Considered whether the gap is informational (board has been briefed and
accepts the residual risk for the 2026-Q3 launch window); ruled out --
acceptance was conditional on the appetite being extended before launch,
not after.
