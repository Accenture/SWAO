# EDM01 -- Ensure Governance Framework Setting and Maintenance

## Verdict (consultant-asserted)

outcome: SATISFIED
severity: high
rationale: >-
  An IT governance charter is in place: approved by the board on 2025-09-12,
  reviewed at the 2026-Q1 board meeting. The charter names the IT Steering
  Committee, the CIO, and the Enterprise Risk Committee as the decision
  bodies; the supporting RACI matrix covers strategy, portfolio, security
  and operations decision rights. Policy hierarchy (Policy -> Standard ->
  Procedure -> Work Instruction) is documented in the policy register. The
  control objective is met at COBIT 5 Process Capability Level 3
  (Established process).

## Evidence references

- ADR-IT-GOV-001 -- IT governance charter (board-approved, 2025-09-12)
- Policy register PR-2026-Q1 -- 47 policies, hierarchy documented
- Board minutes 2026-Q1 -- governance review item 4.2
- RACI-IT-DECISION-RIGHTS v3.1

## Counter-hypothesis considered

Considered whether the steering committee operates only on operational
items (which would weaken the governance claim); ruled out -- the 2026-Q1
agenda included strategy refresh, risk-appetite confirmation and
benefits-realisation review, demonstrating governance-level activity.
