# Security Awareness Training Programme (HRS-02) -- consultant-furnished context

## Verdict (consultant-asserted)

outcome: PARTIAL
severity: high
rationale: >-
  A general security awareness module is included in the onboarding process
  for new employees. However, the training has not been refreshed in over
  two years and does not cover cloud-specific threats. Annual refresher
  training is not mandatory and completion rates for voluntary refreshers
  are below 40%. There is no phishing simulation programme. Contractor
  personnel are not covered. The control partially satisfies HRS-02; the
  annual mandatory refresh, cloud-specific content, and contractor coverage
  are outstanding.

## Counter-hypothesis considered

Considered whether the onboarding training module satisfies the "regular
intervals" requirement given its comprehensive content. Rejected: HRS-02
requires periodic refreshers at defined intervals, not a one-time
onboarding event. The two-year gap and the absence of a mandatory cadence
are clear gaps.
